# Classical Roman Architectural Orders & Modular Proportions

Proportions and geometric rules for authentic Roman architecture (Vitruvius principles).

---

## 1. Classical Orders & Column Height Ratios

Order | Column Height / Lower Diameter | Fluting | Capital Style | Typical Roman Context
:--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---
**Tuscan** | 7 : 1 | Unfluted (Smooth) | Simple round disc | Military castra, utility gates, aqueduct piers
**Roman Doric** | 8 : 1 | 20 flutes or smooth | Square abacus + echinus | Basilicas, Colosseum lowest tier, forum perimeters
**Ionic** | 9 : 1 | 24 flutes | Double spiral volutes | Temples (Fortuna Virilis), library colonnades
**Corinthian** | 10 : 1 | 24 flutes | Acanthus leaves + volutes | Imperial temples (Mars Ultor, Pantheon), triumphal arches
**Composite** | 10 : 1 | 24 flutes | Corinthian leaves + Ionic volutes | Imperial triumphal monuments, central plazas
